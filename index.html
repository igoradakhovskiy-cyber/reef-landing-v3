Is the $1,000 booking refundable?

Yes. It fixes your unit and price, and counts towards the first payment.

What exactly do I own?

The ownership structure, title documents and contract terms are in the presentation, and our manager goes through them with you before any payment beyond the booking.

Can I sell my unit before handover?

Resale and assignment before handover depend on the purchase contract. The contract terms come with the presentation, and a manager will walk you through what applies to your unit.

Does buying give me residency?

Units priced from $100,000 qualify to apply for a Tanzanian residence permit for you, your spouse and children. It is an immigration status, not citizenship or tax residency.

Can I stay as much as I want?

Yes. There is no limit on how long you, your family or friends stay. The dates you don’t use can go into the rental programme.

How am I paid for the rentals?

Life Office, the resort’s management company, handles guests, cleaning, check-in and maintenance. The programme terms, the management fee and payouts are set out in the management agreement, which comes with the presentation.

What about power, water and the internet?

Three power sources (the grid, backup generators and solar), its own well and desalination with reserve tanks, and central cooling. Two separate internet links, satellite and mobile, with Wi-Fi across the resort. Guards, CCTV and keycard access around the clock, and one control room watching it all.

Is the beach club free for owners?

Owners get the Priority card at Pulse Bay: special prices on sport, wellness, the bar and restaurant, and priority booking for the best spots and events. You and your guests pay only for what you use.
